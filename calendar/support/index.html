Support

Contact

Email bastak@gmail.com with your question, the device you use (iPhone, iPad, Mac or Android) and a screenshot if something looks wrong. You’ll get a reply, usually within a couple of days (Brisbane time). If you’re testing through TestFlight, you can also send feedback from the TestFlight app.

Getting started

  • Try it first, if you like. On the first screen tap “Try it with examples”. You’ll see example people and events on this device only. No account needed.
  • Sign in with Apple, Google or Microsoft — from the first screen, or later in Settings → Account.
  • Start a household, or join one. Choose “Start my own” to name your household (and add Health and Work if you want them), or “I’ve been invited” if someone has already added your email.
  • Add people in Settings → People. Pick Home, Work or Health, add the person, choose their role and type their email to invite them. Kids don’t need an email — just add their name. Crews can also add someone straight from the crew editor.

Your calendars

  • Calendars on your device: Settings → Your calendars → Add a calendar → Calendars on this iPhone / iPad / phone. Allow access, tick the calendars, and choose where they go, who they show as and who can see them. Changes come in by themselves.
  • Add by link: paste a calendar’s private iCal (.ics) link and press Check, then Add. It refreshes every 15 minutes.
    • Google: calendar.google.com → ⚙ Settings → your calendar → “Secret address in iCal format”.
    • Outlook / Microsoft 365: outlook.office.com (or outlook.live.com) → ⚙ Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar → “Can view all details” → copy the ICS link. A newly published calendar can take up to an hour to fill.
    • iCloud: Calendar app → ⓘ next to the calendar → Public Calendar → Share link.
  • Connected calendars are read-only in Tak Calendar — change events where they came from.

Questions

Can I be in more than one household?

Yes. Tap the household name at the top of the sidebar to switch, or choose “New household…” or “New business…” to start another.

I was invited but I can’t see the household

Invitations are matched by email. Ask the person who invited you to check they used exactly the email you signed in with, then tap “Check again”. If you used Sign in with Apple and chose “Hide My Email”, sign out and sign in with Google or Microsoft, or with Apple using “Share My Email”.

Who can see what?

Family members see Home. Crew members see only Work, and only their own crews’ jobs. Health is private: each person, the owner and adults of that Health area, and the carers they’ve added.

How do notifications work?

Turn them on in Settings → Notifications and choose what you want. Reminders are set on your device; alerts between people come through push notifications. If nothing arrives, check your device’s Settings → Notifications → Tak Calendar.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → Delete account…, then confirm. Households you own on your own are deleted with everything in them; you’re removed from households other people own. This can’t be undone.
