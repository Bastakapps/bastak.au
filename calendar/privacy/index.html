Privacy Policy

Tak Calendar · last updated 26 October 2026 · applies to Tak Calendar for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android

The short version

  • Tak Calendar is a shared calendar for your Home, Work and Health. What you add is shared only with the people you invite.
  • No ads. No tracking. No analytics companies. We never sell or rent your information.
  • Shared calendar information is stored with Supabase, in their Tokyo (Japan) data centre.
  • Photos for the photo frame stay on your device.
  • You can try the app without an account, and you can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account…

Who we are

Tak Calendar is made by Daniel Agah in Brisbane, Australia (“we”, “us”). If you have a question or a worry about your information, email bastak@gmail.com and Daniel will reply. We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988.

What the app keeps, and why

If you don’t sign in

You can look around with example people and events. Everything stays on your device and nothing is sent to us.

If you sign in

You sign in with Apple, Google or Microsoft. We never see your password. From that service we receive your name and email address (with Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email, and we get a private relay address instead). We use these to identify you and to match invitations sent to your email.

The app then stores what you and your household put into it, so everyone you’ve invited sees the same thing:

  • Home: people’s names and colours, events, chores and stars, rewards, meals and the grocery list.
  • Work: crews and their colours, crew members, jobs (titles, dates, places, status, notes) and colours for types of work.
  • Health: medicines and dose times, doses ticked off, appointments, the care team and their access level, the care log, health reminders, and the emergency card (allergies, conditions, medicines, doctor, emergency contacts).

Health information is sensitive, so it’s kept private: only the owner and adults of that Health area, the person themselves, and the carers they’ve added (for as long and as far as their access allows) can see it. Crew members and Home-only family members can’t see Health at all.

You may add people who don’t have a sign-in, such as young children. Please only add other people’s details when you’re entitled to — for example as their parent or carer, or with their agreement.

Calendars you connect

In Settings → Your calendars you can choose to bring in other calendars. For each one you choose the area it goes into, who it shows as, and whether everyone in that household or business sees it or only you.

  • Calendars on your device: with your permission the app reads the calendars you tick (through Apple’s or Android’s calendar access) and copies their events — titles, dates and times — into Tak Calendar so they appear on your other devices and, if you choose, for your household. It never changes or deletes anything in those calendars.
  • Calendars added by link: if you paste a calendar’s private iCal (.ics) link, we store that link so our server can read the calendar every 15 minutes and copy its events in. The link is visible only to you. Anyone who has such a link can read that calendar, so keep it private; you can remove it in the app, and cancel it in your calendar service at any time.
  • “Just me” calendars are visible only to you, even inside your household.

Removing a calendar in Your calendars removes its copied events from Tak Calendar. Tak Calendar doesn’t read your email.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, your device gives the app a push address. We store it with your account so we can tell you when someone in your household changes something (for example “Mum changed Swimming”). Alerts are delivered through Apple’s Push Notification service. Reminders you set (event reminders, medicine due, morning summary) are scheduled on your device itself.

Photos

Photos you choose for the photo frame / wall display are kept on that device only. They are not uploaded.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don’t track you across other apps or websites, and we don’t use analytics or crash-reporting companies.
  • We don’t sell, rent or trade your information, and we don’t use it for marketing.
  • We don’t read your household’s information except when you ask us to help with a problem, or if the law requires it.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase stores the shared calendar information, runs sign-in and runs the server that reads calendars added by link, in their Tokyo, Japan region. Information is encrypted in transit and protected by access rules so each person only receives what they’re allowed to see. Because Japan is outside Australia, this is an overseas disclosure; Supabase handles it only to run the service for us.
  • Apple, Google or Microsoft — whichever you sign in with — confirm who you are.
  • Apple delivers push notifications.
  • The people you invite see what you share in the areas you’ve added them to.
  • We may disclose information if Australian law requires it.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Your information is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete account… When you delete your account:

  • your sign-in, your person record, your connected calendars and your notification addresses are deleted;
  • any household, business or health area where you are the only owner is deleted with everything in it, including people in it who have no sign-in of their own;
  • you are removed from households that other people own (what you added there stays with that household).

Deleted information can stay in our hosting provider’s routine backups for a short time until those backups are replaced. Anything kept only on your device (examples, photos) is removed when you delete the app. You can also ask us to delete your information, or to see or correct it, by emailing bastak@gmail.com.

Children

Tak Calendar is a family app. Children can be added by a parent or carer, and in Health a child who signs in sees only their own health information. We don’t knowingly let children set up their own household without a parent or carer.

Keeping it safe

Connections are encrypted, sign-in is handled by Apple, Google or Microsoft, and the database only gives each person the rows they’re allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so if we ever learn of a breach that’s likely to cause serious harm, we’ll tell the people affected and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, as the law requires.

Questions or complaints

Email bastak@gmail.com. We’ll reply within 30 days, usually much sooner. If you’re not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is important.
