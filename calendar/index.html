Tak Calendar

One calendar your family shares, in three parts: Home, Work and Health. Everyone sees what matters to them, and nothing more.

Home

Family events in Day, Week and Month views, chores with stars and rewards, meals and the grocery list, and a photo frame for the wall.

Work

A Monday-to-Sunday jobs board for a small business. Give each crew and each type of work its own colour, and put several crews on one job. Crew members see only their own jobs.

Health

Medicines to tick off, appointments, a care log and an emergency card, private to each person and the carers they choose.

Your calendars

Bring in the calendars on your iPhone, iPad or Android phone, or add a Google, Outlook or iCloud calendar by link. They stay up to date by themselves.

Several households

Belong to more than one family or business and switch between them. Each one is completely separate.

Private by design

No ads, no tracking, never sold. Delete your account any time from inside the app.

Tak Calendar is in testing for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android.
