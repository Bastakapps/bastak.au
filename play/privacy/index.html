Privacy Policy

Bastak Play · Last updated 5 November 2026

Bastak Play is a media player for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Android phones and tablets, Android TV and Google TV, and LG and Samsung smart TVs, made by Daniel Agah ("we"), Brisbane, Australia. This policy explains what the app stores and where it connects. In short: everything stays on your device, and we don't collect any of it.

What the app stores on your device

  • Playlists you add: the name, server address and username. Passwords and playlist links are kept in your device's secure storage (the Keychain on Apple devices, encrypted storage on Android). On smart TVs they are kept in the app's own storage on the TV.
  • Media servers you add or sign in to (for example Jellyfin, Emby or Plex): the name, address and username, and the sign-in key the server gives the app. Passwords are only used to sign in and are not saved.
  • Your activity in the app: favourites, podcast subscriptions, saved books, Recent, Continue Watching and how far you got through each video, episode or chapter.
  • Copies of your lists: to open faster, the app may keep a copy of your channel, film and series lists on the device. The copy never leaves the device and is replaced when the list is refreshed.
  • Your profiles and settings, such as theme, preferred audio and subtitle languages and your parental PIN.

None of this is sent to us. We don't run any servers for the app and you don't need an account with us.

Who the app connects to

  • The sources you choose: your playlist provider, your media servers, the servers and computers on your home network, and the web links you enter. The app connects to them directly to show and play your videos and music.
  • Plex: if you sign in to Plex, the app connects to plex.tv to link your account with a code and find your servers. Plex's own privacy policy applies.
  • Free directories you browse: Radio Browser (radio stations), Apple's public podcast directory and each podcast's own feed, LibriVox and the Internet Archive (audiobooks and the free Blender films). When you search, your search words are sent to that directory.
  • Your local network (iPhone and iPad): if you allow it, the app looks for computers and drives with file sharing turned on, so you can browse them.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) (iPhone and iPad): when you open a film or series from a playlist, the app may look up its title on TMDB to show posters, cast photos and episode details. Only the title and year are sent. TMDB's own privacy policy applies to that request.
  • Apple: if you use AirPlay or Picture in Picture, the video is handed to Apple's built-in player on your device.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • We don't show ads or include third-party tracking or analytics.
  • We don't see what you watch, and we don't provide or recommend any content.

Deleting your data

Remove playlists and servers in Settings › Sources (on iPhone and iPad: Settings › Manage playlists and Home › Network), and clear Recent on the Home screen. Deleting the app removes its data from the device. On iPhone and iPad, remove your playlists and servers in the app first to make sure saved passwords are removed too.

Children

Bastak Play is not directed at children. Parents can set up a Kids profile, which hides adult content and needs the parental PIN to leave.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
