Privacy Policy

Bastak Play · Last updated 16 October 2026

Bastak Play is an iPhone and iPad video player made by Daniel Agah ("we"), Brisbane, Australia. This policy explains what the app stores and where it connects. In short: everything stays on your device, and we don't collect any of it.

What the app stores on your device

  • Playlists you add: the name, server address and username. Passwords and playlist links are kept in your device's secure Keychain.
  • Network servers you add or sign in to: the name, address and username. Passwords are kept in the Keychain.
  • Your viewing history in the app: favourites, Recent, Continue Watching and how far you got through each video. Recent stores titles and addresses without any passwords.
  • Your settings, such as preferred audio and subtitle languages.

None of this is sent to us. We don't run any servers for the app and you don't need an account with us.

Who the app connects to

  • The sources you choose: your playlist provider, the servers and computers on your home network, and the web links you enter. The app connects to them directly to show and play your videos.
  • Your local network: if you allow it, the app looks for computers and drives with file sharing turned on, so you can browse them.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB): when you open a film or series from a playlist, the app may look up its title on TMDB to show posters, cast photos and episode details. Only the title and year are sent. TMDB's own privacy policy applies to that request.
  • Apple: if you use AirPlay or Picture in Picture, the video is handed to Apple's built-in player on your device.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • We don't show ads or include third-party tracking or analytics.
  • We don't see what you watch, and we don't provide or recommend any content.

Deleting your data

Remove playlists in Settings › Manage playlists, saved servers in Home › Network, and clear Recent on the Home screen. Deleting the app removes its data from the device. To make sure saved passwords are removed too, remove your playlists and servers in the app first.

Children

Bastak Play is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
