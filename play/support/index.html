Bastak Play – Support

Contact

Email bastak@gmail.com with your question, the device you use (iPhone or iPad) and a screenshot if something looks wrong. We usually reply within one business day (Brisbane time).

Play from your home network

Bastak Play finds computers and NAS drives that have file sharing turned on. On a Mac:

  1. Open System Settings › General › Sharing and turn on File Sharing.
  2. Click the ⓘ next to it, then Options…, and turn on Share files and folders using SMB.
  3. Under Windows File Sharing, tick your user account and enter your Mac password. This lets Bastak Play sign in.
  4. In Bastak Play, open Home › Network, tap your Mac and sign in with your Mac's account name and password.

If nothing appears, check that your iPhone or iPad is on the same Wi-Fi, and that Bastak Play is allowed under Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network. You can also add a server by address, for example 192.168.1.20 or smb://nas.local.

Playlists

  • Bastak Play doesn't provide channels or subscriptions. Use the Xtream login details or M3U playlist link from your own provider.
  • If a playlist won't load, the app shows the reason (for example a wrong password, an expired subscription or a server that can't be reached). Check the details with your provider.

Web links

Paste a link that points straight at a video or stream (for example ending in .mp4 or .m3u8). Links to web pages on video sites such as YouTube can't be played.

AirPlay and Picture in Picture

Tap AirPlay in the player to open the video in Apple's player, which supports AirPlay and Picture in Picture. This works for MP4, MOV and HLS (.m3u8) videos that Apple's player can open. For other videos, use Screen Mirroring from Control Center.

Removing your data

Remove playlists in Settings › Manage playlists, saved servers in Home › Network, and clear Recent on the Home screen. Deleting the app removes everything else it stored.
