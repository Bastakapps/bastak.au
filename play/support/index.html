Bastak Play – Support

Contact

Email support@bastak.au with your question, the device you use (for example iPhone, Android phone, Google TV, LG or Samsung TV) and a screenshot if something looks wrong. We usually reply within one business day (Brisbane time).

On your TV

  • Use the arrows to move, OK to choose and Back to go back. On the home screen, Exit (bottom right) closes the app.
  • During a video, press OK to show the controls. Press Up to reach the progress bar, then Left/Right to move through the video and OK to jump there.
  • Colour keys: Red changes the audio track, Green the subtitles (including Off), Yellow the speed, and Blue adds or removes a favourite.
  • Typing: press OK on a text box to open the TV keyboard, and Back to close it.

Play from your home network

On iPhone and iPad, Bastak Play finds computers and NAS drives that have file sharing turned on. On a Mac:

  1. Open System Settings › General › Sharing and turn on File Sharing.
  2. Click the ⓘ next to it, then Options…, and turn on Share files and folders using SMB.
  3. Under Windows File Sharing, tick your user account and enter your Mac password. This lets Bastak Play sign in.
  4. In Bastak Play, open Home › Network, tap your Mac and sign in with your Mac's account name and password.

If nothing appears, check that your iPhone or iPad is on the same Wi-Fi, and that Bastak Play is allowed under Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network. You can also add a server by address, for example 192.168.1.20 or smb://nas.local.

Playlists

  • Bastak Play doesn't provide channels or subscriptions. Use the Xtream login details or M3U playlist link from your own provider.
  • Large lists open faster the second time: the app keeps a copy on your device and refreshes it in the background.
  • If a playlist won't load, the app shows the reason (for example a wrong password, an expired subscription or a server that can't be reached). Check the details with your provider.

Media servers

Add your own Jellyfin or Emby server in Watch › Add Jellyfin or Emby with its address (for example http://192.168.1.20:8096), username and password. On iPhone, iPad and Android you can also sign in to Plex with a code at plex.tv/link.

Radio, podcasts and audiobooks

These work straight away. Stations come from the free Radio Browser directory, podcasts from Apple's public directory and each show's own feed, and audiobooks from LibriVox. If one station won't play, it may be offline or may not allow other apps; try another.

Web links

Paste a link that points straight at a video or stream (for example ending in .mp4 or .m3u8). Links to web pages on video sites such as YouTube can't be played.

AirPlay and Picture in Picture (iPhone and iPad)

Tap AirPlay in the player to open the video in Apple's player, which supports AirPlay and Picture in Picture. This works for MP4, MOV and HLS (.m3u8) videos that Apple's player can open. For other videos, use Screen Mirroring from Control Center.

Removing your data

On Android and TVs, remove playlists and servers in Settings › Sources. On iPhone and iPad, remove playlists in Settings › Manage playlists and saved servers in Home › Network, and clear Recent on the Home screen. Deleting the app removes everything else it stored.
