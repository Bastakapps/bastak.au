Bastak Play

One player for your own videos: on your device, on your home network, at a web link or in your playlist. For iPhone and iPad.

Your files

Play videos from your device and iCloud Drive, or choose “Open in Bastak Play” from Files, Mail, Safari and AirDrop. MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV and more.

Home network

Finds Macs and NAS drives with file sharing on, then browses their folders. FTP, SFTP and NFS servers can be added by address.

Web links

Paste the address of a video or stream, including HLS (.m3u8), RTSP and RTMP.

Your playlists

Add your own Xtream or M3U playlist from your provider, with channel groups, a search and favourites.

Pick up where you left off

Continue Watching and Recent on the Home screen, and every video resumes from where you stopped.

Subtitles & audio

Switch audio and subtitle tracks, load your own subtitle file, fix subtitle timing, and the app remembers the languages you prefer.

AirPlay & Picture in Picture

Send compatible videos to your Apple TV or AirPlay TV, or keep watching in a small window.

Sleep timer

Stop playback after 15 to 90 minutes, so the video doesn't play all night.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Passwords are kept in your device's Keychain.

Bastak Play is a video player. It doesn't include, sell or recommend any channels, films or other content. You choose what to play and are responsible for having the right to play it.
