Personal Admin — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026 · Contact: support@bastak.au

Personal Admin is made by Daniel Agah (sole trader), Brisbane, Australia ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: we don't run any servers, and we don't receive your data. The app works on your iPhone and talks directly to the services you connect.

What the app reads, and why

With your permission, and only for the accounts and features you turn on:

  • Email (Microsoft Outlook/365, Google Gmail, iCloud, Yahoo and other IMAP accounts): sender, subject, date, preview text and mailing-list headers, to sort your inbox and suggest clean-ups. For emails that look like bills, receipts, bookings or deliveries, the app also reads the message text and small PDF attachments on your device to find amounts, due dates, tracking numbers and similar details. It opens other attachments only when you tap them.
  • Cloud drives (OneDrive, Google Drive): file names, sizes and checksums, to find duplicate files. File contents are not downloaded.
  • Photos on your iPhone: to find duplicates, screenshots, big videos and blurry shots, using small thumbnails processed on the device, and to save smaller copies of videos or Live Photos when you ask.
  • Contacts on your iPhone: to find and merge duplicate contacts.
  • Camera (only when you tap Scan a receipt): to photograph a paper receipt. The text on it is read on your iPhone with Apple's built-in text recognition.
  • Calendar (only if you turn on the household calendar): to add and update the events described below.
  • Folders you pick in Files (iCloud Drive, On My iPhone, connected drives): to find big files and duplicates, read-only.

Where it goes

  • Everything above is processed on your iPhone. Requests go straight from your iPhone to Microsoft, Google, your email provider or Apple. Nothing passes through us.
  • No email, photo, file or contact is sent to any AI service. Summaries and reply drafts are made on your device (with Apple Intelligence where available).
  • We use no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we never sell or share your information.

What is stored, and where

  • Sign-in tokens and app passwords: the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • Extracted facts (for example "Bright Energy bill, $142.30, due 3 Nov"), your categories, rules, snoozes and Undo history: protected files on your device, excluded from device backups. The app does not store email bodies or links found in emails.
  • Before merging contacts, the cards involved are backed up on your device so you can undo.
  • Life vault: documents you add (licence, rego, passport, policies) keep only their type, holder, issuer, dates and the last 4 characters of a number. An optional photo you add is stored on your device with full iOS file protection, excluded from backups, and deleted with Erase all data. Nothing in the vault is uploaded.
  • Paper receipts: a receipt you scan keeps the supplier, date, total, GST and ABN read from it, plus the photo, stored on your device with full iOS file protection, excluded from backups, and deleted when you delete the receipt or Erase all data. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Waiting on: to spot emails that haven't been answered, the app reads your Sent folder with the same read-only permission as your inbox and keeps only the subject, the first recipient, the date, the conversation and whether the email asked something — never its text.
  • Tax pack: worked out on your device from your receipts and statements; the PDF/CSV is created only when you share it, to whoever you choose.
  • Spaces: which space (Personal or a business you name) each email account belongs to is stored on your device.
  • Household calendar (optional): if you turn it on, the app writes bills, renewals and appointments to the calendar you choose, so anyone you share that calendar with can see them. Each event holds only a company or document name, a date, an amount and a reminder — never email subject lines or text. The app only changes or removes events it created, and turning the option off removes them. Calendar sharing itself is handled by Apple (iCloud) or your calendar provider.
  • Files & iCloud Drive scan: when you pick a folder, the app reads file names, sizes, dates and content checksums on your device to find large files and exact duplicates. Results stay on your device, and the app never moves, uploads or deletes your files.
  • Home Screen widgets and Siri receive only names, dates and amounts — never email subject lines.
  • Erase all data (Settings) deletes everything the app stored and signs out of every account. Disconnecting an account removes that account's data.

Changes to your accounts

The app changes things only when you ask: archive, move to Trash or mark email as read; save a reply as a draft; create a mailbox rule you choose to save in Outlook or Gmail; move duplicate files to your drive's bin; move photos to Recently Deleted; merge contacts. Each can be undone. The app never sends email and never permanently deletes anything.

Subscriptions

Personal Admin Plus is sold through Apple. Apple handles payment and gives us only anonymous sales reports; we never see your payment details or Apple ID.

Notifications

Reminders, the Morning Brief and snooze alerts are scheduled on your device. They show company names, dates and amounts — not email subject lines.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Google API Services disclosure

Personal Admin's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features you see in the app, is processed on your device, is not transferred to us or any third party, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

Your choices

You can disconnect any account in the app, revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions or account.live.com/consent/Manage, turn off permissions in iOS Settings, or delete everything with Erase all data. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side — but you're welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.
