Personal Admin — Support

Contact: support@bastak.au — we reply within 2 business days (Brisbane time).

Quick answers

Where do I start? Settings ▸ Guide explains every feature — refunds, bills, subscriptions, inbox, storage, receipts, tax and more — in three steps, with a button that opens it. Each screen also shows a short tip the first time; Settings ▸ Show tips again brings them back.

How do I add a paper receipt? Today ▸ Your admin ▸ Receipts ▸ Scan a receipt. It's read on your iPhone; check the total and category, then Save. The photo stays on your device.

Will the app delete my email? No. It archives, moves to Trash or marks as read — only when you confirm — and you can undo for 30 days in Settings ▸ Recently cleaned. It never permanently deletes and never sends email.

Where did my photos go? Removed photos and videos are in the Photos app ▸ Albums ▸ Recently Deleted for 30 days. Favourites are never removed. Videos you shrank keep a smaller copy in your library with the same date, place and albums.

Can I undo a contact merge? Yes, for 30 days: Clean ▸ Contacts ▸ Recent merges ▸ Undo.

My work account says I need admin approval. Your organisation controls which apps can read work email. Ask your IT team to approve "Personal Admin" (it uses Mail.Read; clean-up asks for Mail.ReadWrite).

How do I cancel Plus? iPhone Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions ▸ Personal Admin Plus. We remind you 2 days before a free trial ends. Restore: Settings in the app ▸ Restore purchases.

What's free? Everything that clears clutter — inboxes, sorting, clean-up, unsubscribe, duplicates, photos, contacts, Timeline, receipts, snooze, parcels, widgets and Siri. Plus adds automation (Morning Brief, auto-tidy, Catch me up) and heavy-lifting tools (exports, shrink videos, Live Photo stills, merge all contacts).

Is my data sent anywhere? No. There are no Personal Admin servers. See the Privacy Policy.

A parcel link looks wrong. Personal Admin never opens links from delivery emails — "Track" always goes to the carrier's own website using the tracking number. If an email asks you to pay a redelivery or customs fee, it's very likely a scam.

How do I erase everything? Settings ▸ Erase all data on this device. Your mailboxes, photos and contacts are not affected.

Reporting a problem

Email support@bastak.au with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version (Settings ▸ About) and what happened. Please don't send screenshots that show private email content.
