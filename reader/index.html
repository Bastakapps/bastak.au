Plans, tracing and proof – in your pocket

Construction plans, cable and fibre tracing, OTDR checks and photo evidence in one app for telecommunications crews.

See what it doesSupport

Built for the field

Everything a crew needs between the van and the pit.

📐

Read & mark up

Open PDFs, plans and documents. Highlight, add notes, sign and search.

🧵

Trace a cable

Follow a cable across sheets from start to end, with pit sizes, works and lengths.

🔬

Trace a fibre & OTDR

Trace a fibre on an SLD to the exchange or to the houses it feeds, and check OTDR traces against the nbn limits.

🧭

Take me there

See where you are on the plan and get directions to any pit or piece of equipment.

📷

Field camera

JPEG photos stamped with GPS, address, ID, date and notes, checked against the plan.

☁️

Your own cloud

Photos and notes upload to your own Google Drive folder and are shared across your crew. Nothing in your Drive is ever deleted by the app.

📴

Works offline

Everything is saved on the device first and uploads when there is signal.
