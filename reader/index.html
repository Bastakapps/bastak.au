Bastak Reader

Construction plans, cable tracing and photo evidence in one app for iPhone and iPad.

Read & mark up

Open PDFs, plans and documents. Highlight, add notes, sign and search.

Trace a cable

Follow a cable across sheets from start to end, with pit sizes, works and lengths.

Take me there

See where you are on the plan and get directions to any pit or piece of equipment.

Field camera

JPEG photos stamped with GPS, address, ID, date and notes, checked against the plan.

Your own cloud

Photos, status and notes upload to your own Google Drive folder and are shared across your crew.

Works offline

Everything is saved on the device first and uploads when there is signal.
