Google Drive

  • Sign in from the camera: Settings › Google Drive › Sign in with Google, then choose your photos folder.
  • Photos upload automatically. An orange cloud with a number in the camera shows photos still waiting.
  • To remove the app's access to your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.
