Privacy Policy

Bastak Reader · Last updated 1 October 2026

Bastak Reader is an iPhone and iPad app made by Daniel Agah ("we"), Brisbane, Australia. This policy explains what the app stores and where it goes.

What the app stores

  • Documents you open, import or scan (PDFs, plans, scans, receipts). They are stored on your device only.
  • Field photos and records: photos you take in the app with their date and time, GPS position, street address, equipment ID, description, job number and the name you enter, plus the status and notes for each item.
  • Location: used while the app is open to show where you are on a plan, give directions and stamp field photos. It is not tracked in the background and is not sent anywhere except inside your own field photos and records.

Google Drive

If you sign in with Google (Settings › Google Drive), the app uses your Google account to upload your field photos and records to the Google Drive folder you choose, and to show the photos and records others using the same account have uploaded. The app only creates, reads, updates and moves to the bin the files and folders it needs for this. Your Google sign-in is kept in your device's secure Keychain. You can sign out in the app at any time, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Bastak Reader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we don't do

  • We do not run servers for the app. Your documents, photos and records go only to your device and to the cloud storage you choose.
  • We do not sell or share your data, show ads, or include third-party tracking or analytics.
  • We do not use your data, including data from Google, to train AI models.

Photos app

If you turn it on in the camera settings, the app also saves copies of your photos to the Photos app on your device.

Deleting your data

Delete photos or records in the app (photos already in Google Drive are moved to the Drive bin). Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. Files in your Google Drive stay under your control.

Children

Bastak Reader is a work app and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
