Privacy Policy

Bastak Reader · Last updated 19 October 2026

Bastak Reader is an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac made by Daniel Agah ("we"), Brisbane, Australia. This policy explains what the app stores, where it goes, and what it never does.

What the app stores

  • Documents you open, import or scan (PDFs, plans, OTDR traces, scans, receipts). They are stored on your device only.
  • Field photos and records (iPhone and iPad): photos you take in the app with their date and time, GPS position, street address, equipment ID, description, job number and the name you enter, plus your notes for each item. They are kept in the app on your device. Photos you save as "This iPhone only" are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Location: used only while the app is open, to show where you are on a plan, find plan items near you, give directions and stamp field photos. It is not tracked in the background. It leaves your device only inside field photos and records you upload to your own Google Drive, and when the app asks Apple Maps for the map picture around you (see below).

Camera, motion, text reading and Face ID

  • Camera: to take field photos, scan documents and show plan items in augmented reality (AR). Motion sensors are used for AR and to know which way the phone is held.
  • Reading labels and text: serial numbers, cable markings and scanned text are read on your device using Apple's built-in text recognition. Spoken descriptions are turned into text on your device where your device supports it; otherwise Apple's speech service may be used.
  • Face ID: if you turn on the app lock, Face ID or your passcode is checked by your device. The app never sees your face data.

Maps

The field home and "Take me there" use Apple Maps. To show the map or satellite picture around you, your approximate location is sent to Apple, as with any map app. Tapping Directions opens Apple Maps or Google Maps, whose own privacy policies then apply.

Google Drive

If you sign in with Google (Settings › Google Drive), Bastak Reader uses your Google account to:

  • upload your field photos and records to the Google Drive folder you choose, filed by job and equipment ID, creating sub-folders there as needed;
  • show the photos and records others using the same Drive have uploaded, so the crew can see what has been done;
  • browse your Drive folders so you can pick where photos go, and (on Mac) open job files such as plans, workbooks and OTDR traces from your Drive.

The app never deletes anything in your Google Drive. Your Google sign-in is kept in your device's secure Keychain. Data from Google goes only between your device and Google – we run no servers and never receive it. You can sign out in the app at any time, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Bastak Reader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we don't do

  • We do not run servers for the app. Your documents, photos and records go only to your device and to the Google Drive you choose.
  • We do not sell or share your data, show ads, or include third-party tracking or analytics.
  • We do not use your data, including data from Google, to train AI models.

Photos app

If you turn it on in the camera settings, the app also saves copies of your field photos to a "Bastak Field" album in the Photos app on your device.

Deleting your data

Deleting a photo or record in the app removes it from your device only; a copy already in Google Drive stays there, and you can delete it in Google Drive yourself. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. Files in your Google Drive always stay under your control.

Children

Bastak Reader is a work app and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
